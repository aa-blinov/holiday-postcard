Scope: single-page postcard studio — primary target `index.html`. Mode: Operate. Audience: someone writing a short winter greeting in English or Russian. Job: pick one of eight stamps, write a message, address the card, download a PNG. Proof/content: authored bilingual sample greetings and eight authored stamp artworks (synthetic demonstration content). Constraints: high-contrast dark terminal-inspired palette; red primary font and red state accent per the user's follow-up; every label ≥4.5:1; preview equals export; no fabricated testimonials or claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dispatch terminal that treats a holiday card as a transmission to be addressed, stamped and sent. The user's follow-up pins a red font: signal red carries the primary voice, selection and status, while ice-white keeps secondary hierarchy. It refuses the soft scrapbook and the decorative command prompt alike: normal inputs and buttons, one authored postcard.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black blue-charcoal ground and darker card paper with a fine grid; pale ice-white secondary ink; signal red #ff6b6b as the primary font, accent, selection and status color, with deep red #c21f2e inside the printed stamp. Dense monospaced type, hairline rules, squared corners, one-pixel red registration marks. The stamp block stays pale paper with dark printed ink so the red system never becomes monotone.

STORY: The visitor sees the finished card first and understands three steps, stamp, words, address, in the rail beside it. The EN/RU switch translates the whole studio and the card's printed labels and survives a reload. Four authored winter scenes rotate every seven seconds, pausing while the visitor edits, and the stage bar names the current one. Editing updates the card immediately; a quiet status line confirms each change. Download saves exactly the previewed card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop slim header with brand, EN/RU switch and status, then a stage bar naming the current background scene and a two-column workbench: right rail (roughly 360px) with eight enlarged stamp previews in a 4×2 grid, message field, To/From fields and Download PNG; left side dominated by the postcard. Nothing important below the fold at 1440×900. On mobile the card leads, controls follow in one column and the scene tag is hidden.
