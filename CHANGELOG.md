# Changelog

All notable changes to holiday-postcard. Versions are ordered newest first.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Nothing yet.

## 0.5.3 — 2026-11-26

### Changed

- Snowstorm gained layered snowfall, wind gusts and lit cabins.
- Forest gained three depth planes of pines, mist bands and a cabin.
- Railway gained a train, station canopy, telegraph wires, signals and distant hills.

## 0.5.2 — 2026-11-26

### Changed

- The footer greeting now uses an authored SVG snowflake as its separator; the leading red square is gone.

## 0.5.1 — 2026-11-26

### Changed

- The footer now shows a rotating localized greeting instead of the sample-content and local-only notice.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-26

### Added

- Eight distinct thematic backgrounds: observatory, snowstorm, city, forest, aurora, railway, celebration and lanterns.

### Changed

- Removed the visible top status pill and bottom status line; status messages now live in a screen-reader-only node.
- Vertically centred the brand mark with its wordmark and subtitle.
- Moved celebration garland and hanging lanterns clear of the greeting text.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-26

### Added

- Four authored winter backgrounds rotate every seven seconds; editing pauses the rotation.
- A localized scene tag in the stage bar names the current composition.
- EN/RU choice is saved in `localStorage` and restored after reload, including the default greeting.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-25

### Changed

- The card background gained concentric moon rings, aurora arcs, varied snow, pines and lit cabins.
- The printed GREETING / ПРИВЕТСТВИЕ caption was removed from the card; the form label remains.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-25

### Added

- Eight stamp designs with enlarged chooser previews.
- Russian and English interface and card text, with six sample greetings per language.
- Signal-red primary font and state colour, verified against the 4.5:1 floor.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25

### Added

- The first working studio: live canvas preview, message and address fields, PNG export at 2400×1500.
